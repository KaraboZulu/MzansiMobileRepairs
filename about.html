About Us

Our History

Mzansi Mobile Repairs started in 2022 in Soweto with one goal: to make quality phone repairs affordable for everyone. What began as a small repair table has grown into a trusted shop helping over 2000 customers fix their devices.

Our Mission

To provide fast, reliable, and affordable mobile phone repair services while building trust in our community.

Our Vision

To be South Africa's most trusted mobile repair brand by 2030.
